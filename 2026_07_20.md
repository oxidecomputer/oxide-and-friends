# Oxide and Friends: July 20th, 2026

## The Past, Present, and Future of Digital Storytelling with Ramy Katrib

We've been hosting a live show weekly on Mondays at 5p for about an hour,
and recording them all; here is
[the recording](https://youtu.be/-M2kKpsxdfg).

Ramy Katrib has been at the forefront of digital storytelling for over 25
years, with his post-production company, DigitalFilm Tree, earning a reputation
as one of Hollywood's most technically adventurous post-production houses.
He joined Bryan and Adam to explore the past, present, and future of digital
storytelling.

[Bryan Cantrill](https://bsky.app/profile/bcantrill.bsky.social) and
