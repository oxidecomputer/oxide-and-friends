# Oxide and Friends: July 27th, 2026

## The Open Weight Revolution with Simon Willison

We've been hosting a live show weekly on Mondays at 5p for about an hour,
and recording them all; here is
[the recording](https://youtu.be/f_6TzmifxnI).

Simon Willison joined Bryan and Adam to discuss a wild couple of weeks in AI.
First, we have a high-profile, AI-induced, AI-diagnosed security incident. Next
we have the rise of frontier-class open-weights models. Who's for or against
open weights? Do they make for a more or less secure future? Exciting times!

In addition to
[Bryan Cantrill](https://bsky.app/profile/bcantrill.bsky.social) and
[Adam Leventhal](https://bsky.app/profile/ahl.bsky.social),
our special guest was
[Simon Willison](https://bsky.app/profile/simonwillison.net).
