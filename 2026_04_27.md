# Oxide and Friends: April 27th, 2026

## Mechanical Engineering at Oxide

We've been hosting a live show weekly on Mondays at 5p for about an hour,
and recording them all; here is
[the recording](https://youtu.be/sF0xVSenfGk).

In addition to
[Bryan Cantrill](https://bsky.app/profile/bcantrill.bsky.social) and
[Adam Leventhal](https://bsky.app/profile/ahl.bsky.social),
we were joined by Oxide Colleages
Elliott Donlon,
Brooks Willis,
Doug Wibben,
and Ben Williams.

Bryan and Adam were joined by members of the Oxide mechanical engineering team
to talk the mechanical challenges of building a rack-scale computer, and--in
particular--of scaling manufacturing from just a few racks to hundreds.
