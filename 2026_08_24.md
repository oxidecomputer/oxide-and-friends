# Oxide and Friends: August 24th, 2026

## Emulating Oxide: How Faux Can You Go?

We've been hosting a live show weekly on Mondays at 5p for about an hour,
and recording them all; here is
[the recording](https://youtu.be/RdH7oQ1ddcA).

How do you test configurations you haven't built yet? Steve, Andrew, and Rain join Bryan and Adam to talk about the transformative power of the Oxide emulation environment, VOXEL, and how it has accelerated development in many domains.

In addition to
[Bryan Cantrill](https://bsky.app/profile/bcantrill.bsky.social) and
[Adam Leventhal](https://bsky.app/profile/ahl.bsky.social),
speakers included
[Steve Karam](https://bsky.app/profile/did:plc:utigqr27poprcprsvihwu65m),
Andrew Stone,
and [Rain Paharia](https://bsky.app/profile/sunshowers.io).
