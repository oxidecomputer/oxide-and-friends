# Oxide and Friends: October 12th, 2026

## Oxide's $445M Series D

We've been hosting a live show weekly on Mondays at 5p for about an hour,
and recording them all; here is
[the recording](https://youtu.be/hhTO8d0R3to).

After raising $200M in February... we raised another $445M in July! Your
favorite computer company is capitalized to the gills--enough to weather the
supply chain upheaval even! Come for co-founders Steve and Bryan describing the
raise; stay for tax, debt, and credit talk with Scott Orn.

In addition to
[Bryan Cantrill](https://bsky.app/profile/bcantrill.bsky.social) and
[Adam Leventhal](https://bsky.app/profile/ahl.bsky.social),
our special guests were
[Steve Tuck](https://bsky.app/profile/sdtuck.bsky.social)
