# Oxide and Friends: March 30th, 2026

## Building a Quorum of Trust in the Oxide Rack

We've been hosting a live show weekly on Mondays at 5p for about an hour,
and recording them all; here is
[the recording](https://youtu.be/Cn9hACi9S5o).

The Oxide rack contains within it a distributed system that needs to trust
itself. But how is this trust built? Bryan and Adam were joined by colleagues
Andrew and Finch to explore how Trust Quorum was designed, built, and verified.

In addition to
[Bryan Cantrill](https://bsky.app/profile/bcantrill.bsky.social) and
[Adam Leventhal](https://bsky.app/profile/ahl.bsky.social),
we were joined by Oxide colleagues,
Andrew Stone, and Finch Foner.
