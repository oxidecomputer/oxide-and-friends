# Joining Oxide and Friends

Welcome! Guests are what makes the show--no one likes hearing Bryan and Adam
just yammer at each other. Here are a few tips:

- There **is** a live chat for the show in Discord... read along, but don't let
chat distract you! We've had guests try to multi-task--speaking and reading--it
doesn't go great.

- We have a live audience, but most listeners will consume the show later
(after edits) on YouTube or the pod. **Referring to chat or images in chat
isn't great listening and/or means we have to clean it up in the edit.**

- Interruptions happen: internet connectivity, Discord split-brain, dogs
barking, sirens, trumpeters (yes really). The best way to handle this for the
edit is to reset. Pause, gather your thoughts, and start over. We'll clean it
up in the edit and you'll sound brilliant.
