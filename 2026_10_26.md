# Oxide and Friends: October 26th, 2026

## Books in the Box VI

We've been hosting a live show weekly on Mondays at 5p for about an hour,
and recording them all; here is
[the recording](https://youtu.be/59C8fnKGywg).

It's the annual Oxide and Friends tradition: Books in the Box! What books have the Oxide friends enjoyed? Didn't catch the show live, feel free to add your book recommendation as a PR in the show notes.

In addition to
[Bryan Cantrill](https://bsky.app/profile/bcantrill.bsky.social) and
[Adam Leventhal](https://bsky.app/profile/ahl.bsky.social),
speakers included
[Tom Lyon](https://mastodon.social/@aka_pugs),
and [Ian Grunert](https://hachyderm.io/@iangrunert).
